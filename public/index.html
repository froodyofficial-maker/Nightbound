Survivors

Repair generators to power the exit gates, open a gate and escape before the night closes in.

  • WASD move · Shift run · Ctrl/C crouch
  • Hold E: repair, heal, free caged survivors, open gates, snuff lanterns
  • Space: vault, drop barricades on the killer, hit calibration checks
  • When carried, alternate A / D to struggle free
  • Two hits put you down. Caged a third time and you're gone.

Killer

  • LMB lunge strike · RMB power
  • Hold E: pick up, cage, damage generators, break barricades
  • F execute a downed survivor who's been caged twice (or the last one standing)
  • Space vault · Q drop survivor · R Lamplighter's Shadow Step

The Killers

  • Butcher: Rampage charge (2 charges) · sees blood trails
  • Wisp: Fade invisibility · ambush lunge · chilling frost
  • Harrower: 3 bone spears · marks his prey
  • Lamplighter: cursed lanterns reveal survivors · teleports between them
  • The Foreman (OFFAL crossover): Lights Out darkness · hunts by sound · one-hit hook in the dark
